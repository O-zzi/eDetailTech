Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

eDetail Tech ("eDetail Tech", "we", "us") operates an internal WhatsApp-based communication platform used to support engagement between pharmaceutical company representatives and healthcare professionals (doctors), including campaign messaging, educational content delivery, and sample request handling.

Data we access

Through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), we send and receive WhatsApp messages with healthcare professionals who have engaged with our platform, including message content, phone numbers, delivery/read status, and message template interactions.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively to operate the messaging platform: routing conversations, logging doctor engagement and sample requests, tracking campaign delivery, and enabling representatives to follow up appropriately. Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to unrelated third parties.

Data storage and retention

Conversation data and related records are stored in a private, access-controlled database and are retained to support ongoing engagement history and reporting, unless deletion is specifically requested (see Contact below).

Third-party processors

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to send and receive messages, and a customer conversation management platform to operate the messaging inbox. These providers process data solely to deliver the messaging service on our behalf.

Revoking access

A healthcare professional or representative may stop receiving messages at any time by replying to opt out, or by contacting us directly using the details below.

Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact eDetail Tech at info@thesportscollective.org.
